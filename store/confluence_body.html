"AWS 3-year is cheaper"True at the extreme: AWS H100 3yr is $2.97 no-upfront / $1.38 100%-prepaid. But that locks 3 years and full prepayment. Nebius 2yr $2.05 needs no 3rd-year lock or 100% upfront, and on-demand has no commitment at all. Sell flexibility, not the headline rate.high
