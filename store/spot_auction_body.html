"Market floor reference" = the lowest real competitor spot/auction signal observed (hyperscaler spot median, SF Compute market price, or a field-intel deal) — the closest proxy for where competitor auction/spot clears, and a sensible anchor for a PVM target price. The cost-based floor (electricity + overhead, ~$0.40 for Hoppers in discussion) is a separate, lower bound. Hyperscaler spot is interruptible with no capacity guarantee; field-intel deals are sales-reported and often committed, not pure spot. SF Compute is one of the few public spot-market exchanges (H100 only so far; B300 "coming this fall").
