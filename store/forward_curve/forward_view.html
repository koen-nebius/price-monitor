How to read it

Marks are weighted medians of two evidence classes kept visible separately: competitor offers reported by sales (deduplicated; skews to losses) and Nebius achieved prices from CRM (aggregates only). They are not a bid and an ask; no spread is implied. Public multi-year contracts are shown as a reference and never pooled. Observations are shifted to today's price level by quarter of quote (factors in the footer; the recent only switch removes that adjustment) and re-expressed at the chosen prepay basis with Finance's money-cost convention (about 3.45% per year of tenor at full prepay, concave in the share). Offers that do not state prepayment are counted at 0% unless the stated prepay only filter is on.

Filled points have six or more observations with recent support. Hollow points are thin (three to five). Cells under three observations are suppressed, never interpolated.

Backwardation means the 36-month mark sits under the 12-month mark: the market pays less per hour for a longer lock. Commitment length alone is not a delivery-date curve; these are committed-price benchmarks at quote date. Internal only. Never quote marks to customers.
