How to read it

Marks are weighted medians of every dated observation for a GPU and commitment length: competitor quotes reported by sales (the bid side, skews to losses) and Nebius signed reserve deals from CRM (the ask side, aggregates only). Public multi-year contracts (SemiAnalysis deal table) are a third, half-weighted leg. Everything is shifted to today's price level; the prepay basis re-expresses every point at the chosen prepayment using Finance's grid convention (about 3.4% per year of tenor at full prepay, concave in the share), and the quotes filter keeps only observations whose own prepayment sits in that band.

Filled points have six or more observations with recent support. Hollow points are thin (three to five). Cells under three observations are suppressed, never interpolated.

Backwardation means the 36-month mark sits under the 12-month mark: the market pays less per hour for a longer lock. Internal only. Never quote marks to customers.
