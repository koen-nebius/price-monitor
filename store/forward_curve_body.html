Bid vs ask by cell

A large positive spread on a cell where we still win says the market pays for our availability/quality; a negative spread says we are leaving money on the table.

Observations in window: 132 bid quotes (field intel, latest 2026-09-11), 128 ask cells covering 283 signed deals (reserve_tenor.csv generated 2026-09-15).

  1. Prepay normalisation: p₀ = p ÷ (1 − 0.06 × prepay share). Calibrated between the Nebius AE grid (30%→100% prepay = −3% on B300 12m, −7% on GB300 36m) and the CoreWeave H100 ladder (25%→100% = −4.4%).
  2. Quote-date normalisation: two-way fixed effects on log p₀ (tier×tenor cell + quote quarter, pooled across tiers); each observation is shifted to the as-of quarter. Estimated quarter effects (log, vs as-of): 2025Q3 -0.40, 2025Q4 -0.43, 2026Q1 -0.41, 2026Q2 -0.20, 2026Q3 +0.00. Pooling across tiers is a v1 simplification; Hopper and Blackwell repriced by similar ratios in 2026H1.
  3. Weights: observations ≤ 120 days old count 1, ≤ 365 days count ½, older are dropped; ask cells weigh min(deals, 3) so a single mega-deal cannot dominate.
  4. Mark: weighted median of adjusted prices per cell; suppressed below 3 observations, never interpolated or carried forward.
  5. Tenor buckets: ≤4 → 3m, ≤8 → 6m, ≤14 → 12m, ≤20 → 18m, ≤27 → 24m, ≤42 → 36m, longer → 60m. Sanity band $0.50–$15.00.
  6. Not modelled yet (v2): delivery-date axis (forward start vs immediate), cluster size, region/interconnect, per-family time effects, credit quality. The curve is a term structure at quote date, not a delivery-month strip.
