  1. Prepay normalisation: discount = 0.034 × years of tenor × (prepay share)^0.5 (capped 25%); every quote is expressed at 0% prepay. This is Finance's own grid convention (sheet "." of Pricing model.xlsx: 12m −3.4/−2.63/−1.8% and 24m −6.97/−5.26/−3.57% for 100/50/30% prepay). Nebius CRM deals carry no prepay percentage, so payment type is used as a proxy: upfront = 100%, prepaid monthly = 8%, postpaid = 0%.
  2. Quote-date normalisation: two-way fixed effects on log p₀ (tier×tenor cell + quote quarter, pooled across tiers); each observation is shifted to the as-of quarter. Estimated quarter effects (log, vs as-of): 2025Q3 -0.37, 2025Q4 -0.43, 2026Q1 -0.40, 2026Q2 -0.20, 2026Q3 +0.00. Pooling across tiers is a v1 simplification; Hopper and Blackwell repriced by similar ratios in 2026H1.
  3. Weights: observations ≤ 120 days old count 1, ≤ 365 days count ½, older are dropped; ask cells weigh min(deals, 3) so a single mega-deal cannot dominate; public announced contracts weigh ½ (implied rates assume 8,760 billed hours).
  4. Mark: weighted median of adjusted prices per cell; suppressed below 3 observations, never interpolated or carried forward.
  5. Tenor buckets: ≤4 → 3m, ≤8 → 6m, ≤14 → 12m, ≤20 → 18m, ≤27 → 24m, ≤42 → 36m, longer → 60m. Sanity band $0.50–$15.00.
  6. Not modelled yet (v2): delivery-date axis (forward start vs immediate), cluster size, region/interconnect, per-family time effects, credit quality. The curve is a term structure at quote date, not a delivery-month strip.
