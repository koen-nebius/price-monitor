What this monitor cannot see

  • Provider utilization: 'sold out' means the public self-service shelf at list price is empty; capacity may be allocated to reserved/private deals.
  • Hyperscaler on-demand stockouts (AWS/GCP/Azure ICE) are not externally observable; AWS is proxied via spot pools until the Capacity Blocks IAM lands.
  • Marketplace depth (Vast) measures listed supply, not datacenter inventory; falling depth = demand absorbing supply OR hosts delisting.
  • Self-reported badges (GMI) are marketing statements and can rot.
  • Levels are weak evidence; transitions and trends are the signal. Trend layer activates at 7 days of history (day 38/7).
