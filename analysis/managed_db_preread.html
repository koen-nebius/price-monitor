Context

This pre-read gives the market pricing picture and the structural choices, so the meeting can focus on the commercial model rather than data gathering.

Structural facts that shape the decision

  • Aiven's full price matrix is a public no-auth API (api.aiven.io/v1/service_types: every plan × cloud × region with hourly rate, specs, backup config). A Nebius substrate would literally be a new column in this matrix. We snapshot this API daily in the price monitor as of 2026-08-15, so movement is tracked from now on.
  • Substrate spread is precedented and large: the same Valkey plan is $110 on DigitalOcean, $140 on AWS, $165 on GCP, $250 on Azure. Aiven already prices aggressively on value substrates — the DigitalOcean column is the natural template for a Nebius column.
  • Aiven's markup structure: ~2.0× raw compute for caches, ~2.5× for PostgreSQL (estimate basis: plan price vs equivalent bare VM). Relevant for any revenue-share / substrate-rate negotiation.
  • Valkey is structurally cheaper than Redis: AWS prices Valkey -20% (nodes) to -33% (serverless) below Redis OSS and adds end-of-life surcharges (+80–160%/node) to old Redis versions. New SKUs should anchor to Valkey-era rates, not legacy Redis.
  • Pricing-model norm: flat per-service-hour T-shirt plans, everything bundled (backups/PITR, egress, TLS, monitoring); HA bought as a plan-family step. Clean 2× HA at AWS/DO/GCP; Aiven charges 2.3–2.7× on GCP/Azure. Per-service-hour with clean 2× HA is both the market norm and the simplest metering.
  • Free tiers: Aiven keeps perpetual free plans off the big-3 clouds (DigitalOcean only for Kafka; small free PG/MySQL/Valkey on AWS regions) — a deliberate lead-gen vs revenue split we can copy or skip.

Open questions for the meeting

  • Engine scope: Valkey only at launch, or PG/MySQL/Kafka in the same wave?
  • Commercial model: resell on Nebius paper vs Aiven list with Nebius substrate vs BYOC (Aiven BYOC = customer pays infra + custom management fee, commitment required)?
  • Who owns the customer-facing list price, and does the Aiven agreement constrain our floor?
  • Metering unit for the SKUs (per-service-hour flat, matching the market norm?) — aligns with the metering/SKU work already running with Liubov Pavlova.
  • Free/developer tier stance.
